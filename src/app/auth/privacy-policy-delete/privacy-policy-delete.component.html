How to delete your account

You can delete your account from within MTA Konnect.

Deleting your account is irreversible. We can't reverse this even if you delete it by accident

To delete your account

  1. Open MTA Konnect.

  2. Tap more options > Menu > Profile > Delete my account.

  3. Tap Delete account.

Deleting your account will:

  • Delete your account from MTA Konnect and all your devices

  • Erase your team, project and groups details

  • Delete you from all of your MTA Konnect groups.

  • You will be removed from all of your MTA Konnect groups.
